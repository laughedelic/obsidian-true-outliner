Two open choices in the drag preview. In every card the same run is in flight, an ordered item third with two children, held at the seam between first and second. The ghost is drawn where it will land; the item itself is lifted further down.

1. The ordered item's placeholder

The number an item lands with is the renumbering's answer, not the item's — the run's own start at the head of a list, the previous item's plus one after it — so the ghost draws a placeholder rather than predicting it. Each candidate is shown enlarged, at its real 13.6px size beside a bullet, and on the seam. #. is shown beside the heading mark it could be read as when heading marks are drawn as hashes.

2. The count at the rule's end

How many nodes are in flight — here third and its two children, three — as a total rather than what the ghost hides, since a run can be several roots and …1 for two dragged nodes reads as one of them. Nothing is drawn for a lone node. B is what the beta draws: the rule stops as far short of the count as it starts from the ghost.
